Why this guide

Two tax systems, one financial life.

The United States taxes its citizens and green card holders on worldwide income regardless of where they live. Thailand assesses personal income tax on foreign-source income that tax residents remit into the country. Living between these two parallel systems means your finances are subject to two separate sovereign rulebooks at once.

The objective is not simply to file two tax returns. It is to align residency, remittance timing, income characterization, and recordkeeping before cross-border movement creates avoidable tax exposure.
