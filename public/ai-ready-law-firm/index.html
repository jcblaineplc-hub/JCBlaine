Why this guide

AI is not just a tool decision. It is a law firm business-model decision.

Most law firms are approaching AI through pilots, vendor demos, and informal associate-level experimentation. That is not enough. The real question is whether the firm can redesign how legal work is governed, produced, priced, and staffed.

This guide gives firm leaders a practical first sequence: establish guardrails, select high-value workflows, build a knowledge foundation, test pricing changes, and prepare lawyers for a different leverage model.

Inside the guide

The five pillars of the AI-ready law firm

The roadmap is organized around five operating pillars that need to advance together. A firm that buys tools without redesigning these pillars will likely see isolated efficiency gains, not durable business-model change.

01

Governance

Define who may use AI, for what work, under what supervision, and with what documentation.

02

Workflow

Choose repeatable legal processes where AI can change cycle time, staffing, and client experience.

03

Knowledge

Turn firm know-how into governed, reusable assets that support quality and consistency.

04

Pricing

Use efficiency gains to test fixed fees, subscriptions, retainers, and outcome-oriented offerings.

05

Talent

Redefine training, leverage, review standards, and partner expectations for AI-assisted work.

90-day roadmap

A focused first move, not a firm-wide science project.

The recommended path is intentionally narrow: create governance, pick the right workflows, run controlled pilots, then convert what works into pricing and operating-model decisions.

Days 1-30

Set the guardrails

Stand up an AI steering group, create interim policy, classify use cases, and select pilot matters.

Days 31-60

Redesign selected workflows

Map current work, test AI-assisted steps, measure time and quality, and document human review points.

Days 61-90

Turn pilots into a business model

Decide what to scale, what to stop, how to price, and what talent and knowledge changes are required.
